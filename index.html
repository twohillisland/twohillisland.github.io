The Story

One boy could change the course of a secret war.

Fifteen-year-old Matthew Mac Iver wants to be more than the troublemaker his father thinks he is. When his older brother is sent on a secret military mission, Matt follows — and ends up on the HMNZS Kea, bound for a remote island off New Zealand's coast.

But the island hides a dark secret. When the mission unravels, Matt finds himself cut off, hunted, and forced to survive with only his wits and courage.

From the stormy seas of Tolaga Bay to the treacherous hills of Two Hill Island, Matt's fight for survival becomes a test of loyalty, family, and what it truly means to be brave.

About the Author

Maurice Matthews

Maurice Matthews is a New Zealand author with a passion for bringing history to life for young readers. Two Hill Island draws on extensive research into New Zealand's wartime coastline, the vessels that patrolled it, and the young men — and boys — who found themselves caught up in a conflict far larger than anything they had imagined.

Maurice believes that the best stories for young people combine real historical truth with characters who feel genuinely flawed, brave, and human. Matt Mac Iver is not a born hero. He is a boy trying to prove himself — and that, Maurice argues, is exactly what makes him unforgettable.

Two Hill Island is Maurice's debut novel for young readers.
